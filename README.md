# SPSS – Product Placement & Impulse Buying Behaviour

## Project Overview

This project studies the influence of product placement on impulse buying behaviour among college students in retail stores.

Primary data were collected through a structured questionnaire and analysed using IBM SPSS.

The study uses statistical techniques to examine relationships, prediction and differences between selected variables.

## Research Topic

**A Study on the Influence of Product Placement on Impulse Buying Behaviour Among College Students in Retail Stores**

## Objectives

- To examine the relationship between attractive product displays and impulse purchases.
- To examine the relationship between exceeding the planned shopping budget and regret from impulse purchases.
- To determine whether purchasing after prominent product placement predicts impulse purchases.
- To examine the combined effect of selected product-placement variables on impulse purchases.
- To compare the overall product-placement influence score with the neutral value.
- To compare Product Placement Score and Impulse Buying Score.

## Dataset

- Sample size: 50 respondents
- Data source: Primary questionnaire
- Software: IBM SPSS
- Significance level: 5%

## Statistical Analysis

| Analysis | Variables |
|---|---|
| Pearson Correlation | Q7 & Q19 |
| Spearman Rank Correlation | Q13 & Q20 |
| Linear Regression | Q11 → Q19 |
| Multiple Regression | Q7, Q11, Q17 → Q19 |
| One-Sample T-Test | Q30, Test Value = 3 |
| Paired-Samples T-Test | Product Placement Score & Impulse Buying Score |

## Key Results

### 1. Pearson Correlation

**Variables:** Q7 & Q19

- Pearson correlation = -0.014
- Significance = 0.925
- N = 50

The relationship was not statistically significant at the 5% level.

### 2. Spearman Rank Correlation

**Variables:** Q13 & Q20

- Spearman correlation = -0.101
- Significance = 0.486
- N = 50

The relationship was not statistically significant.

### 3. Linear Regression

**Independent Variable:** Q11  
**Dependent Variable:** Q19

- R = 0.268
- R² = 0.072
- Significance = 0.060

The regression model was not statistically significant at the 5% level.

### 4. Multiple Regression

**Independent Variables:** Q7, Q11 and Q17  
**Dependent Variable:** Q19

- R = 0.281
- R² = 0.079
- Significance = 0.282

The overall regression model was not statistically significant.

### 5. One-Sample T-Test

**Variable:** Q30  
**Test Value:** 3

- Mean = 3.06
- t = 0.444
- df = 49
- Significance = 0.659

There was no statistically significant difference from the neutral value.

### 6. Paired-Samples T-Test

**Pair:** Product Placement Score vs Impulse Buying Score

- Product Placement Score Mean = 2.94
- Impulse Buying Score Mean = 2.92
- Mean Difference = 0.02
- t = 0.29
- df = 49
- Significance = 0.776

There was no statistically significant difference between the two scores.
