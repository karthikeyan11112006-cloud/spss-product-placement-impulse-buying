# 📊 Dataset

This folder contains the dataset collected for the research project.

## Research Topic

**A Study on the Influence of Product Placement on Impulse Buying Behaviour Among College Students in Retail Stores**

## Dataset Details

- **Sample Size:** 50 respondents
- **Data Collection Method:** Google Forms
- **Target Respondents:** College Students
- **Study Variables:** 30

## Variables Included

- Product placement-related variables
- Impulse buying-related variables
